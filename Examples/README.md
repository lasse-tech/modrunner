# Example modules

Four MED modules, included so the player has something to play and the test
suite has something to check against.

| File | Format | Blocks | Sequence | Instruments | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `Happy Hour.med` | MMD0 | 15 | 27 | 14 | 3:27 |
| `Magic Noises.med` | MMD0 | 12 | 22 | 10 | ~2:50 |
| `Take it slow.med` | MMD0 | 15 | 33 | 9 | ~4:15 |
| `Terminator II.med` | MMD0 | 20 | 32 | 13 | ~4:05 |

All four are 4-channel MMD0, tempo 33 at 6 ticks per line (125 BPM).

## Authorship

These are compositions by **Lars Gossard**, written in 1993 with MED V1.30 and
originally released as the *Magic Noises* collection. They are included here by
the author.

They are **not** covered by the Apache-2.0 licence that applies to the source
code. The collection's original terms were: freely distributable, provided no
profit is made, the songs are not altered, and the author is credited. Being the

The files are the 1993 originals, byte for byte; only the `.med` extension was
added, since Amiga files carried no extensions.
